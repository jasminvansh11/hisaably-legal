Hisaably — Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 21, 2026

Hisaably ("Hisaably", "we", "us", or "our") is a personal finance management application designed to help users record and manage expenses, income, budgets, savings goals, bills, recurring expenses, and personal Udhar records.

1. Information We Handle

Hisaably may handle information that you choose to enter into the app, including:

  • Expense information such as amount, title, category, date, payment method and notes.
  • Income information such as amount, source, category, date and notes.
  • Budget information.
  • Savings goal information.
  • Bill and recurring-expense information.
  • Friends & Udhar records, including names, amounts, notes and settlements.
  • Profile information that you voluntarily provide.
  • App settings such as language, currency and theme.

Please do not enter passwords, bank account passwords, card PINs, OTPs or other authentication credentials into Hisaably.

2. Local Data Storage

Hisaably is designed as an offline-first application. Your financial records are primarily stored locally on your device using the app's local database.

Hisaably does not require an account to use its core offline functionality.

If you uninstall the application, locally stored data may be removed. Hisaably cannot guarantee recovery of locally stored data after app deletion, device failure or loss of the device.

3. Data Sharing

Hisaably does not sell your personal financial records. Financial records stored locally are not intentionally transmitted to Hisaably servers for core offline functionality.

Third-party services used by the application, such as advertising and payment services, may process information according to their respective policies.

4. Advertising

The free version of Hisaably may display advertisements through third-party advertising services such as Google AdMob.

Advertising providers may process information such as advertising identifiers, device information, IP address and interaction information according to their applicable policies.

5. Hisaably Plus

Hisaably may offer an optional paid subscription called "Hisaably Plus".

Subscriptions are processed through Google Play Billing. Hisaably does not directly collect or store your complete payment-card details.

Subscription pricing, renewal, cancellation and refunds are subject to the applicable Google Play terms.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by Hisaably. Because core financial records are stored locally on your device, you are also responsible for protecting access to your device.

No electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

You can manage and delete locally stored financial information through the application.

Hisaably may provide a "Clear All Data" feature that permanently deletes locally stored app data.

Deleted local data may not be recoverable.

8. Children's Privacy

Hisaably is intended for general personal finance management. We do not knowingly request personal information from children for account creation or personalized services.

9. Third-Party Services

Hisaably may use services such as:

  • Google Play Services
  • Google Play Billing
  • Google AdMob
  • Other libraries required for app functionality

These services may process information according to their respective privacy policies and terms.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the application, services, legal requirements or data practices change.
